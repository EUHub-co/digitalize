# EUHub AI Editorial and Evidence Policy

## Purpose

EUHub AI publishes practical material for teams planning, buying or operating AI systems in regulated European environments. Content must help a reader make a decision; it is not published to manufacture search visibility or to replace legal, security or procurement advice.

## Authorship and review

- Every high-stakes guide must name its author and reviewer only after both identities, roles and public profile links are approved for publication.
- A subject-matter reviewer checks technical accuracy. A qualified legal/privacy reviewer checks EU AI Act, GDPR, data-transfer, contractual and security claims where those claims are made.
- Each published guide displays its reviewed date. A material correction updates that date and records the change in the source content.
- EN, Slovak and German versions require fluent human review for meaning, terminology, calls to action and cited sources before release. Machine assistance may support drafting or translation, but never substitutes for that review.

## Sources and claims

- Prefer primary sources: legislation, regulator guidance, official standards and vendor documentation for specific product behavior.
- Cite a source where a reader should be able to verify a factual, regulatory or technical claim. Links must describe the source rather than use generic anchor text.
- Do not present a design option, anticipated result, model behavior or benchmark as a delivered client outcome.
- Client names, logos, quotations, architecture details and attributable metrics require written approval. If approval is unavailable, use an anonymized and clearly qualified evidence note or omit the claim.

## AI assistance and corrections

- AI may be used to assist research organization, drafting, translation and quality checks. A responsible human remains accountable for published material.
- Readers can report an error through the contact form. Material corrections are reviewed promptly and the relevant page's reviewed date is updated after the correction is published.
- Editorial content does not create a legal opinion, security certification, contractual promise or guarantee of regulatory compliance.

## Structured data

- Structured data describes content that is visible on the corresponding page.
- FAQ schema is used only for real, visible questions and answers. It is not a promise of rich-result display.
- Author, reviewer, Organization, Person, Article and case-study properties are omitted until the underlying public facts are approved and verifiable.
